# Decisions and deviations

Assumptions and changes from the design doc, newest last.

1. **Project lives at the repo root.** The doc's layout uses a `dust-and-mandibles/` folder; here `D:\DustAndMandibles` itself is the Godot project, so `project.godot` sits at the root.
2. **Input actions are registered in code** (`autoload/InputSetup.gd`) instead of the `[input]` section of `project.godot`. Hand-writing serialized InputEvents is error-prone, and this keeps all bindings in one readable place. Adds one autoload not named in the doc.
3. **Canyon runs along Z.** West plateau at x < -22.5, east at x > 22.5. Playable plateau is 40 m along Z by 30 m back from the lip; terrain extends further (hills beyond the fence, end walls at z = ±88) so the canyon reads as a landscape.
4. **Lip overhang 2.2 m**, lip band from 26.6 m up, underside 25.4 to 26.6 m. Starting values for the line-of-sight layer of the cross-defense rule; tune in M2.
5. **Lanes at z = -16, -8, 0, 8, 16**, marked by a vertical crack plus ledges at 7, 14 and 21 m.
6. **Vertex colours carry albedo; vertex alpha < 1 marks glowing parts.** Lets each generated prop be one mesh with one shared toon material (fewer draw calls). Colours are converted to linear in `MeshKit.vcol`.
7. **Strata shader uses `cull_disabled` and flips the normal on back faces**, so terrain shades correctly whatever the winding.
8. **Outline width grows with distance** (up to 3x) so bugs stay readable 45 to 60 m away. The optional full-screen edge-detect pass for terrain is deferred.
9. **No custom font yet.** Fonts from Google Fonts would be a download; the HUD uses Godot's built-in font until a Rye/Rubik (OFL) font is added with its license file.
10. **GUT not added in M0.** It's a download (MIT), and M0 has no logic worth unit-testing. It gets added in M1/M2 before the targeting, economy and wave-budget tests.
11. **Canyon fall penalty deferred.** Falling hauls you home after 3 s; the "small penalty" (scrap) needs the M2 economy.
12. **Hits apply locally in M0.** The revolver emits `fired(origin, dir)` so M1 can route it through `request_fire` to the host.
13. **Lobby can start solo.** The game starts automatically once two players are ready; the host also gets a "Start alone (testing)" button (and `--solo` flag) until M2's proper solo test mode.
14. **Tin cans are cosmetic and not synced.** Every peer nudges its own cans when it sees a shot land. Host-authoritative damage only applies to things with `apply_hit` that aren't loose physics props.
15. **Bridge is a two-half rope drawbridge at z = -17**, pivoting at each lip. It lowers in the build (and lobby) phase and hauls up when the warning starts. Each peer animates it from the replicated phase, so it needs no sync of its own.
16. **Placeholder wave length of 30 s** until M2's bugs end the wave. `--fast` scales phase times by 0.15 for testing; `--debug` enables F6 to skip a phase.
17. **Physics layers:** 1 = world (bullets hit it), 2 = player-only blockers (invisible boundary walls).
18. **Shot origin is trusted from the client** (host re-raycasts from it). Fine for co-op; revisit if cheating ever matters.
19. **The cliff face away from the sun sits in shadow tint.** Left as is for now; the M3 day/dusk lighting pass should make sure both far faces read well.
20. **Canyon is 60 m deep (Logan's request), not the doc's 28 m.** Every height (lip band, overhang underside, fall-into-canyon line, end walls) now derives from `WorldLayout.CLIFF_HEIGHT`. Ledges sit every 10 m. Bugs climbing at the doc's speeds now take about twice as long; climb speeds are a tuning knob for M2 playtests. With a 30 degree turret pitch limit, turrets reach roughly the top 28 m of the far face.
21. **Pause menu (Logan's request): Esc opens it in game; the title screen has a Settings button with the same tabs.** The game keeps running behind the menu (it's online co-op); your character just stops taking input. Settings save to `user://settings.cfg`, including key remaps, which override `InputSetup`'s defaults.
22. **Social tab invites are "IP:port" for now** (LAN address of the host, copied to the clipboard). Joining from the tab leaves the current game and joins from the main menu. Proper invites through Steam or a relay would replace this behind the same tab once internet play is added (doc: "Internet play (later)").
23. **Esc no longer frees the mouse directly**; it opens the pause menu, which frees it.
24. **Unit tests use a tiny built-in runner (`tests/run_tests.gd` + `TestCase`) instead of GUT**, because GUT is a download. The tests are written so GUT can replace the runner later.
25. **Turret pitch limit is 30 degrees, not 15.** With a 45 m gap, 15 degrees only reaches the top few metres of the far face; 30 still can't see your own face (the lip overhang hides it and it's nearly straight down). It's a per-turret field in `data/turrets/*.tres`.
26. **Bug loot goes straight into the stockpile of the cliff the bug was climbing** (with a kill-feed line), instead of physical pickups. Keeps "your turrets earn for your partner" without syncing pickups. Pickups can come later.
27. **Bugs emerge at the base of their lane** instead of walking from the nests. The nests are visual for now.
28. **Breachers steer straight at targets over the open plateau** (no NavigationAgent3D). Generated terrain would need runtime navmesh baking; the plateau is open enough for the proof of concept.
29. **Bugs aren't pooled.** They're replicated with MultiplayerSpawner, which creates and frees nodes; VFX are pooled. Revisit if waves of 150 bugs stutter.
30. **Building is allowed in any phase**, not only the build phase. Repairing is allowed for anyone on any turret (paid by the repairer), since helping your partner is the point.
31. **Spitter Scorpions cling at 65% of the cliff height** (the doc says halfway) so turrets can actually reach them on the deeper canyon.
32. **Players have 100 HP, are downed for 15 s at 0 and respawn at home.** Breachers attack players within 7 m first.
33. **Balance pass from automated soak tests (no players shooting):** climb speeds are 1.5x the doc's (the 60 m cliff doubled exposure time), Gatling damage 3.5 at 65% hit chance, and bug HP grows 12% per wave (`hp_per_wave` in `wave_config.tres`). Starting values only; the M2 playtest decides.
34. **Debug automation flags** for testing without a person: `--soak=N` (headless waves, auto-ready), `--autobuild [--light]`, `--showcase` (one of each bug frozen on the west face), `--cam=x,y,z,tx,ty,tz`, `--pause=tab`, `--seconds=N` with `--shot`.
35. **M3 resources are scrap, chitin, water, food and chili.** Corn gives food, prickly pear gives food + water, firecracker chili gives chili (Chili Mortar ammo). The doc's single "crops" resource is split so chili can be ammo.
36. **Water goes straight into the stockpile** when you collect from a well or rain catcher (up to 5 per press = "a bucket"), instead of carrying a physical bucket item.
37. **The well is the Homestead's windmill, broken at the start** (80 scrap to fix, the doc's well cost), then 1 water per 8 s into its trough. Rain catchers are buildable (30 scrap): 1 water per 20 s from dew, 1 per 3 s in a storm. About 30% of build phases from wave 2 on are storms.
38. **Each Homestead has a fixed 3x3 crop field** (a row each of corn, prickly pear, chili). Plant is free; watering costs 1 water and lasts the whole growth; unwatered crops pause. Grow times 150-240 s (scaled with `--fast`).
39. **Hunger and thirst** drain 0.2/s (a full bar lasts about two wave cycles), never kill; below 20 hunger disables sprint and thirst slows reloading 1.6x. F eats 1 food (+35), Q drinks 1 water (+35). Well-fed (hunger > 70) doubles health regen. They live on the owning peer like HP.
40. **Turret ammo is paid by the turret owner's stockpile**: Water Cannon 2 water/shot, Chili Mortar 1 chili/shot. Without ammo the turret idles and says so. This is how farming feeds defence.
41. **Turret upgrades (U while looking at a turret):** tiers 1-3 cost 10/25/50 chitin; each tier gives +25% damage, +10% range, 8% faster fire and +20% HP (fully repaired).
42. **Water Cannon knock-off:** the bug drops back to the canyon floor and takes 2x its height in fall damage (a bug at 50 m takes 100). Chili Mortar leaves a 3 m burning patch for 5 s; Dynamite Lobber splashes 3.5 m with falloff; Lasso Harpoon roots for 3 s.
43. **Traps live behind the turret line** (rows 3+). Barbed wire, bear traps and dynamite crates can be walked over (their own physics layer, 16). Palisades work as chew-walls: breachers attack the nearest structure, so a palisade in their path holds them up.
44. **Burrow Ticks spawn underground on the plateau** (no climb), invisible and unhittable until they are next to their target or inside a lantern's 9 m light. **Dragonfly Bandits** fly from the canyon to a crop field or water source, steal 3 food/water, then fly off and despawn (no loot). **Crop Weevils** climb, then go for planted crops before anything else. **Brood Mothers** burst into 6 spiderlings (10 HP, not in the threat budget) once, at half health or on death.
45. **New bug costs:** Brood Mother 8 (wave 7), Burrow Tick 3 and Crop Weevil 2 (wave 6), Dragonfly Bandit 3 (wave 8). Several bugs can debut in the same wave; each new one appears on both cliffs.
46. **Build menu has 12 entries**: number keys 1-0 pick the first ten, the mouse wheel cycles through all of them in build mode.
47. **Day/dusk:** build phases are morning (storms are grey), warning + wave are orange dusk with a low sun; lanterns switch on at dusk. Music crossfades between a calm guitar loop and a faster wave loop, both made by `tools/audio/make_music.py`.
48. **The canyon continues into the distance (Logan's request)** instead of ending in walls: beyond the playable stretch (|z| > 90 m) it carries on to 700 m in both directions at low detail and without collision, fading into the fog. Players can't get there (boundary walls), so it's scenery only.
49. **The Iron Tumble (wave 10 boss)**: 3000 HP, climbs the heavier cliff's centre lane at 0.8 m/s pushing a boulder; its back plate halves turret damage from across the canyon, its glowing rear belly seam takes double damage from the owner shooting down. At half health it drops off and re-climbs the other cliff, with six spiderlings as escorts. If it crests, the boulder smashes the Homestead for 150.
50. **Polish:** floating damage numbers and a hit-marker X for your own shots (yellow on weak spots), light screen shake on firing and near big blasts, a boss health bar. No hit-stop (time scale changes would desync co-op).
51. **Exports:** Windows and Linux presets are in `export_presets.cfg` (no longer gitignored). Exporting needs Godot's export templates for 4.7.2, which are a separate download.
31b. Spitter Scorpions cling for 25 s, then climb on, so a wave can never stall on one that nobody can reach.
52. **Players are crash test dummies in cowboy hats (Logan's request, placeholder model)**: yellow segmented body with quartered black/yellow markers, team-colour hat band and bandana, procedural walk, aim and slide poses (`scenes/player/dummy_rig.gd`). In first person you see your legs and feet and a dummy hand on the revolver; your head, torso and arms only cast shadows so they don't block the view. `--thirdperson` shows your own full body (for screenshots).
53. **Spitters cling at 78% of the cliff height** (was 65%) so turrets reach them easily; an automated test showed the wave 8 stall came from spitters destroying turrets with acid until none could shoot back, which the 25 s cling limit now prevents.
54. **Builds:** `godot --headless --export-release "Windows Desktop" builds/windows/DustAndMandibles.exe` (and "Linux"). Export templates for 4.7.2 are installed on Logan's PC. `builds/` is gitignored.
55. **Last-line turrets (playtest feedback):** Scattergun Post (55 scrap, 90 damage every 2.4 s, 14 m) and Sharpshooter Tower (70 scrap, 150 damage every 4 s, 32 m). They shoot only bugs on their owner's own plateau (breachers, surfaced Burrow Ticks, Dragonfly Bandits overhead) and never anything on either cliff face (`Targeting.guard_allows`, unit-tested). They can go anywhere on your plateau, including the turret line.
56. **No eating or drinking (playtest feedback).** Hunger and thirst are gone. Water is for watering crops (and Water Cannon ammo). **Sage herbs** are a new crop: hotbar slot 4 uses one herb to heal 50 HP.
57. **Vegetables are separate resources** (corn, prickly pear, chili, herbs) instead of "food", so the vendor can price them separately. Chili is still Chili Mortar ammo (1 per shot) and water is still Water Cannon ammo (2 per shot).
58. **Farm plots are buildable** (10 scrap, behind the turret line). Each Homestead starts with three. On an empty plot, F picks the seed (corn, prickly pear, chili, herbs) and E plants it.
59. **Vendor cart (one per plateau)** buys vegetables for chitin, with a price sign. Base prices: corn 1.0, prickly pear 1.6, chili 1.2, herbs 2.2 chitin. Each unit sold drops that price by 5% of base (floor -50%); prices climb back about 0.4% of base per second while you don't sell (cap +50%). Fractional chitin is credited and paid out in whole units. F picks the vegetable, E sells one, hold E to keep selling.
60. **Gunsmith (one per plateau)** sells the lever-action rifle (15 chitin: 48 damage, 8 rounds, pierces armour) and coach shotgun (20 chitin: 9 pellets x 14 damage, 2 shells, 35 m), and upgrades any weapon twice (10 then 25 chitin; +25% damage and 15% faster reloads per level). Shotgun pellets use a shared random seed so the host and the shooter agree on every pellet.
61. **Hotbar (keys 1-8)** at the bottom of the screen: 1 revolver, 2 lever rifle, 3 shotgun, 4 herbs, 5 build tool, 6-8 free. While the build tool is open, the number keys pick what to build instead.
62. **Build spots are shown as a grid** while building: green tiles where the selected item fits, red where it's taken, the tile under your crosshair brighter. The build hint moved to the top of the screen.
63. **The Oasis (Logan's request):** a stone spire in the middle of the canyon at z = -30 (south of the climb lanes, so it never blocks turret fire), flat-topped at cliff height with grass, a spring pond, palms, shrubs and flowers, a big "OASIS / trading post" sign, and the market: the vendor cart and the gunsmith (moved off the plateaus). Both drawbridges now run from each lip to the spire, and a fenced walkway along each lip leads from the plateau to its bridge. The bridges still haul up at the warning bell, so don't get caught shopping at dusk.
64. **HUD:** the Homestead bars read "Yours" (left) and "Theirs" (right) for each player; scrap and chitin are big counters bottom-left (solo shows both stockpiles as west / east).
65. **Hotbar is weapons + items:** 1-3 guns, 4 water bucket, 5 corn, 6 prickly pear, 7 chili, 8 herbs, with stockpile counts. Selecting the bucket or a vegetable puts the gun away and shows it in your hand; herbs heal (press 8 while hurt). Build mode is on B.
66. **Starter farm field (Logan's request):** 12 bigger plots (2.6 m, with a wooden border) in a 3 x 4 grid beside the house, the 3-plot side along the line of the house front and 4 rows toward the canyon, 1 m paths between them. The water tower, a salvage pile and the tin-can crate moved to make room.
67. **Seeds (Logan's request):** planting uses one seed of that crop, and nobody starts with seeds. The Oasis vendor sells them for scrap on the same counter where you sell vegetables (F cycles through sell and buy lines). A seed costs a third of its harvest's baseline return: harvest yield x base chitin price, converted at 10 scrap per chitin (corn 10, prickly pear 11, chili 16, herbs 15 scrap). See `MarketPrices.seed_price`.
68. **Scaffolding (Logan's request):** a buildable scaffold (25 scrap) on turret-line cells, up to 3 levels (build again on the same spot to add a level). Each level is 3 m and holds a turret on its platform, plus one on the ground inside the frame, so one cell can hold up to 4 turrets. A turret on level N gets 5 x N more degrees of look-down. Hold E on the scaffold to repair its most damaged turret from the ground; aim and press U / X to upgrade or demolish a specific turret; X on the scaffold removes its top level (only if that level is empty). Demolish/repair/upgrade now name the exact node you aimed at.
69. **Hotbar rework (Logan's request, replaces #65):** 1-3 are open weapon slots (the revolver starts in slot 1; new guns fill the first free slot, three at most). 4-6 fill with whatever harvest you carry (corn, prickly pear, chili, with counts). 7 is medicine (herbs, heals 50 HP), 8 the water bucket. Each slot shows a drawn icon of its item; empty slots stay blank. **Q drops the weapon in your hand** (remappable): it lands at your feet, keeps its upgrades, and anyone with a free slot can pick it up with E. The gunsmith won't sell you a fourth gun.
70. **Chili pricing (Logan's request):** a chili seed costs a flat 20 scrap, and a chili harvest sells for about 20 chitin (5 each at baseline, still swinging ±50% with demand). Other seeds keep the one-third rule.
71. **Right-click aim (Logan's request):** hold right mouse to raise the gun to the sights and zoom in (revolver 1.4x, shotgun 1.25x, lever rifle 2.2x). Mouse sensitivity scales with the zoom, you walk at 60% and can't sprint, and aimed shotgun blasts spread 40% less (the aim flag rides on the shot's seed, so no new network message). Right click still leaves build mode.
72. **Build menu (Logan's request, replaces the number-key bar):** B opens a wooden panel on the left with four tabs: Farming (farm plot, rain catcher), Cliff Turrets (the six cliff-face turrets and scaffolding), Tactical (barbed wire, bear trap, dynamite crate, palisade, lantern post) and Last Resorts (scattergun post, sharpshooter tower). Each item shows an icon rendered from its real 3D model, its name, price (red if you can't afford it) and a short description. Click it or press its number: the menu tucks away and you place it. B brings the menu back, the wheel cycles items in the category, right click leaves.
73. **Enterable Homestead (Logan's request):** the cabin is now a real building (gen/house_builder.gd): log walls with a door and window openings, plank floors, a ramp staircase along the back wall with a top landing, a table, stools, stove, bed and crates. A **Homestead Ledger** desk inside (F picks, E buys) sells upgrades: patch the walls (25 scrap, +100 HP), board up the windows (40 scrap, bugs do 30% less damage), reinforce the walls (80 scrap + 10 chitin, iron bands and a stone skirt, +250 max HP), a second floor (120 scrap + 15 chitin, front balcony) and a lookout floor (180 scrap + 30 chitin, needs the second; railed roof deck). Max Homestead HP is now per house (GameState.homestead_max). Stair collision leaves out the painted tread lines (they snagged the player); `--stairtest=N` walks a player up every flight headless and `--ledgertest` buys every upgrade.
74. **House turrets (Logan's request):** Porch Repeater (45 scrap, quick twin gun) and Rooftop Cannon (70 scrap, slow grapeshot splash), listed under Last Resorts. They snap to the house's mounts: 2 on the porch, +2 on the balcony with the second floor, +2 on the roof deck with the lookout floor. They only shoot bugs on your own plateau within 20 m of your house (never cliff faces). Plateau-guard splash now hits bugs on the plateau instead of climbers. `--houseturrets` sets up both houses fully armed (a 240 s soak: 58 cannon kills, 33 repeater kills).
75. **Airship casino (Logan's request):** "The Lucky Mandible" drifts in over the Oasis at the start of the build phase after every 5th wave (wave 6's build phase in a 10-wave run), which then runs 60 s longer. It hovers 14 m above the spire, unrolls a rope ladder (hold forward to climb, step off forward at the top, jump to let go), and has two slot machines (10/25/50 scrap bets; cactus x3, chili x5, horseshoe x8, star x20, two horseshoes return the bet) and a prize wheel (5 chitin a spin: scrap, chitin, chili seeds, herbs or a 25-chitin jackpot, three bust wedges) plus a bar. Every peer animates the ship from the game phase (no sync); the games ask the host. At the warning bell it hauls up the ladder and leaves, setting anyone aboard down on the Oasis. Debug: `--airship` forces a visit, `--laddertest` climbs aboard, plays and checks the departure.
