Audio credits
======
Other recordings
----------------

Most sound effects in audio/sfx are generated by the scripts in tools/audio.
Recorded sounds from free libraries are listed here (source, author, licence,
and what was done to them).

Generator (Waterworks)
----------------------
generator_run.wav   "Generator (loop)" by YCbCr, OpenGameArt
                    https://opengameart.org/content/generator-loop
                    Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
                    4.6 s cut, tail crossfaded over the head so it loops seamlessly.
generator_start.wav "Car engine Start Up 02" by looneybits, OpenGameArt
                    https://opengameart.org/content/car-engine-start-up-02
                    Licence: CC0 1.0
                    Starter crank pitched up 12% (a smaller engine), crossfaded into
                    the generator loop above so it coughs and then catches.
generator_stop.wav  From "Generator (loop)" by YCbCr (CC0, above): slowed to ~55%
                    pitch with irregular dropouts and a fade, so the engine sputters out.

Processing: a one-off Python/numpy script (decode with ffmpeg,
resample, crossfade, gate, normalise to 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz WAV).

AI-generated sound effects (tools/audio/import_fal_sfx.py)
----------------------------------------------------------
Generated with fal.ai, ElevenLabs sound effects v2 (fal-ai/elevenlabs/sound-effects/v2),
from text prompts written for this game (kept with the raw takes in the project archive).
Decoded to mono 44.1 kHz, trimmed, faded, loudness-matched to the sounds they replaced;
loops crossfaded end-over-start. These replace synthesized versions from make_sfx.py,
make_foley.py and make_dynamite.py (those scripts would overwrite them if run again).
  match_strike*, fuse_sizzle* (loops), dynamite*, thunder*, whip_crack_1-3,
  skitter*, beetle_hiss*, spider_screech*, acid_spit*, loot_crack*,
  homestead_hit*, hammer_hit*, build, bell, coin, register,
  land_dirt*, land_wood*, slide*, throw*, spur*, zip_start,
  harvest_pull*, plant_dig*, water_pour, rustle*, pump*, salvage*, hook_bite*, herb_eat,
  cig_light, zippo_open, zippo_close, zippo_strike,
  dice*, card_flick*, roulette_spin, slot_lever
New sounds:
  wing_buzz*      fliers (locusts, dragonflies) arriving
  queen_shriek*   the Hive Queen (was spider_screech pitched down)
  mother_roar*    the Hive Mother (likewise)
  train_chuff.ogg, train_whistle, train_brakes   the Iron Mandible (ch10)
  airship_engine.ogg                             the Lucky Mandible's engines
