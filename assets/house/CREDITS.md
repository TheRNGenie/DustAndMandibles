# House textures

AI-generated with fal.ai (fal-ai/patina/material: z-image texture + PATINA normal
maps), then channel-packed for `shaders/toon_house.gdshader`: weathered wood
planks, chipped pale-blue clapboard, wooden shingles, fieldstone and corrugated
tin. Prompts and the packing script (pack4.py) are kept in
D:\DustAndMandibles-archive\textures.
