# Train, yard and ruin models

The Iron Mandible's locomotive, tender, boxcar and private car (assets/train), the water tower and pump jack (assets/yard, the jack split into stand and walking beam) and two ruined homesteads (assets/story, cut open at the front): AI-generated with fal.ai (z-image/turbo concept art, trellis 3D), cut down with tools/props/prep_ai.py. They stand in for the builder meshes visually (AiModels.stand_in); collision and logic are unchanged.
